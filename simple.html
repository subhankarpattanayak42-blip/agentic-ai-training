DAY 1 · WHAT IS AN AI HELPER?

Plain talk and analogies first — then watch one work, live.

Start Here

Artificial Intelligence, plain and simple

📖 It reads and writes

Like a colleague who has read millions of documents. Ask a question — it answers, in any style you like.

🗣️ It follows instructions

Give a clear request, get a clear answer. Think of briefing a capable, well-read assistant.

✋ It has no hands (yet)

On its own, AI can only talk. To actually do things, it needs tools. That's the whole story of this workshop.

The Heart of the Idea

Agent vs Chat — talking vs doing

💬 AI Talker (a Chat)

  • You ask, it answers
  • Forgets everything between chats
  • Can't actually do anything — no hands
  • Like ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

A brilliant co-worker who only gives advice.

🦾 AI Helper (an Agent)

  • Remembers what you told it
  • Can send email, book meetings, search the web
  • Thinks several steps ahead and finishes the job
  • Like an assistant that acts on your behalf

A focused PA who handles it end-to-end.

Agent = a Chat + a memory + hands + permission to work
What to Look For

Six things that make a real Helper

🌟 Works alone

You set the goal, it finds the way. No hand-holding.

👀 Pays attention

Notices the situation and reacts to the context around it.

🧩 Thinks in steps

Breaks a big task into a clear plan and works through it.

🧠 Remembers

Keeps track of what has been done — even across different days.

🤌 Has hands

Sends emails, updates calendars, opens files, reaches the web.

📈 Learns

Gets better from feedback and from what it experiences.

Day 2 · The Choice

Where can a helper live?

💻 On your laptop

  • Like an app installed on your own computer
  • Private — everything stays with you
  • Works even when offline
  • Best for sensitive work that shouldn't leave your desk

☁️ In the cloud (big shared computers)

  • Runs on a company's large, secure server rooms
  • Can be reached from anywhere, on any device
  • Handles bigger jobs and more people at once
  • Best when several people need it together
Both work. Your choice is about privacy, cost, and how big the job is.
Day 2 · Buy or Build

Ready-made helpers vs building your own

📱 Social media helper

Writes posts, replies, and keeps your X/Twitter account active — so you don't have to.

💼 Personal assistant

Chats with you on WhatsApp or Telegram, runs on your own machine, keeps your data private.

🚀 Self-starter

You name a goal; it figures out the steps and browses the web to finish it on its own.

👥 Team coordinator

Splits one big job between several helpers with different roles that work together.

Day 2 · What They Do

What a helper can genuinely do for you

🎯 Finish jobs on its own

Complete a multi-step task without you supervising every step.

🔗 Connect your tools

Reach your email, calendar, files, and data without you hopping apps.

🧠 Remembers the conversation

Keeps the context, so you don't repeat yourself.

📝 Writes anything

Reports, emails, briefings, and slide decks — drafted in seconds.

🔍 Researches for you

Searches the web and pulls out the useful parts.

🤝 Teams up

Several helpers collaborating on one large, messy problem.

Day 2 · In Your Work

Their day-to-day — made easier

🚪 Visitor management

Fills the gate register, checks each visitor against approved & blacklists, issues the pass, and notifies the host — no paperwork queues.

🪪 Gate pass management

Tracks every pass, flags expired or misused ones early, and renews the routine ones — so lapses don't turn into incidents.

🛡️ Access & clearance checks

Verifies IDs and clearances against the approved lists and raises the alarm on anything that doesn't match.

📋 Guard log & handover

Reads the shift log and drafts a clean handover, highlighting anything the next shift must know.

🗓️ Duty roster & daily orders

Drafts the roster from leave and postings, then reminds everyone of their slot and duties.

🗂️ Unit administration

Personnel records, leave, returns to HQ — filled in and tracked, so admin doesn't pile up.
